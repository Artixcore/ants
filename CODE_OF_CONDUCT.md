# Code of Conduct

Ants welcomes respectful technical collaboration from people of all backgrounds and experience levels.

## Our standards

Participants are expected to:

- communicate with patience, honesty, and professional respect;
- critique ideas, code, and evidence without attacking people;
- disclose uncertainty and mistakes plainly;
- protect private, personal, and security-sensitive information;
- use project channels for relevant technical collaboration;
- follow repository licensing, contribution, and security rules.

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable conduct includes:

- harassment, threats, intimidation, or discriminatory language;
- sexualized attention or unwelcome personal contact;
- doxxing or publishing private information;
- malicious code, poisoned fixtures, intentional secret exposure, or deceptive security reports;
- spam, fraud, impersonation, or deliberate disruption;
- retaliation against someone who raises a good-faith concern.

## Scope

This policy applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, project-managed community spaces, and public conduct when someone is representing Artixcore or Ants.

## Reporting conduct concerns

Report conduct concerns privately to `ismam.ceo@artixcore.com`. Do not post personal, medical, legal, or other sensitive details in a public issue.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, reject contributions, restrict participation, or permanently exclude participants whose conduct violates this policy. Decisions should consider context, severity, history, impact, and the safety of affected people.

Good-faith technical disagreement is welcome. Abuse is not.
