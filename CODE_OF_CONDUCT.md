# Code of Conduct

Ants welcomes respectful technical collaboration from people of all backgrounds and experience levels.

## Expected conduct

Participants must communicate with patience, honesty, and professional respect; critique ideas without attacking people; protect private information and security-sensitive details; avoid harassment, discrimination, spam, and deliberate disruption; and follow repository licensing and contribution rules.

## Unacceptable conduct

Unacceptable behavior includes personal attacks, threats, sexual harassment, discriminatory language, doxxing, malicious submissions, intentional exposure of secrets, or attempts to misuse project channels for fraud or exploitation.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues, pull requests, or other contributions that violate this policy. Repeated or severe violations may result in temporary or permanent exclusion from project spaces.

Report conduct concerns privately through an official Artixcore contact. Do not publish personal or sensitive information in a public issue.
